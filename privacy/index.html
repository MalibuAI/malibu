Privacy policy.

How Malibu collects, uses, and protects personal data across malibu.tech, the console, the provider app, and community channels.

Last updated July 21, 2026
Effective July 21, 2026

01Introduction

Malibu (“Malibu,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is operated by Malibu AI, Inc. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal data in connection with our online interfaces and activities, including:

  • The informational website at malibu.tech and its subdomains
  • The Malibu console at console.malibu.tech
  • The Malibu provider desktop application for macOS
  • Community communication channels administered by Malibu, including forums, GitHub Discussions, and messaging platforms
  • Analytics, support, and technical tools associated with the foregoing (collectively, the “Services”)

This Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party websites, public blockchain networks not controlled by Malibu, or activities conducted entirely outside the Services.

02Important notice regarding blockchain technology

Transactions conducted on public blockchain networks are public, immutable, and irreversible.

When you interact with the Services — for example by paying for inference in USDC, receiving $MALIBU rewards as a provider, or verifying an inference receipt on-chain — certain information may be recorded on a public blockchain, including:

  • Wallet addresses and public blockchain identifiers
  • Transaction hashes, receipts, and related metadata
  • Timestamps and protocol-level interaction data

Malibu does not control public blockchain networks and cannot modify, obscure, anonymize, or delete information recorded on-chain. Any personal data written to a public blockchain or otherwise disclosed through on-chain activity may remain permanently accessible and may be processed by third parties outside Malibu’s control.

This Privacy Policy applies only to personal data collected off-chain through the Services. It does not apply to information publicly available on blockchain networks.

03Personal data we collect

(a) Information you provide voluntarily

  • Email address or other contact information you choose to provide
  • Content you submit through forms, email, support requests, or community platforms
  • GitHub account information (such as username, email address, and avatar) if you sign in to the console with GitHub OAuth
  • Community usernames, profile information, or other content you voluntarily disclose in Malibu-administered forums

(b) Blockchain-related information

  • Public wallet addresses you connect to the Services
  • Transaction hashes and other publicly available blockchain data associated with those wallets
  • Receipts, refunds, and settlement metadata connected to inference activity

Malibu does not control how public blockchain data is recorded, displayed, or indexed. Malibu does not associate wallet addresses with real-world identities unless such information is voluntarily provided by you.

(c) Service usage information

For buyers, we may process:

  • API keys, credit balances, and top-up history
  • Prompt routing metadata (such as the model requested, token counts, response latency, and provider assignment) — not the contents of your prompts unless you have explicitly saved them
  • Console session state, feature usage, and support interactions

For providers running the Malibu Mac application, we may process:

  • The public wallet address used to receive earnings
  • Hardware and OS characteristics your node reports (such as chip family, memory, and macOS version) for scheduling and eligibility
  • Uptime, latency, throughput, and other node-performance metadata
  • Verification data submitted for verified-model settlement

(d) Automatically collected information

When you access the Services, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Device identifiers
  • Pages viewed and interaction data
  • Referring URLs and timestamps

(e) Aggregated or de-identified information

We may create aggregated or de-identified information for analytical and operational purposes. Such information does not identify, and cannot reasonably be used to identify, an individual.

04Information we do not collect

Malibu does not intentionally collect:

  • Government-issued identification numbers
  • Social Security numbers
  • Payment card or bank account information
  • Biometric identifiers
  • Precise geolocation data
  • Health or medical information

The Services are non-custodial with respect to your crypto assets. Fiat top-ups, where offered, are handled by third-party payment processors; Malibu does not itself store card or bank data.

05How we use personal data

We use personal data for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Services
  • To route inference requests, meter usage, and settle payments between buyers and providers
  • To distribute $MALIBU rewards according to network rules
  • To respond to inquiries and communications
  • To monitor, analyze, and improve system performance and security
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity
  • To comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations

Malibu does not use personal data for targeted advertising.

06Cookies and analytics

The Services may use cookies or similar technologies to:

  • Enable core functionality, including session and authentication state
  • Analyze traffic and usage trends
  • Improve user experience and system performance

You may disable cookies through your browser settings. Certain features of the Services may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

07How we share information

Personal data may be disclosed:

  • To service providers that support hosting, analytics, security, communications, or infrastructure
  • To professional advisors, including legal and accounting firms
  • To comply with applicable law, regulation, subpoena, or legal process
  • To protect the rights, safety, and integrity of Malibu, our contributors, users, or others

Malibu does not sell personal data. Malibu does not share personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising.

08Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Enforce agreements

Public blockchain data is retained indefinitely by the applicable blockchain networks and is not subject to our retention controls.

09Your privacy rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have certain rights regarding personal data we process, including the right to:

  • Request access to personal data we maintain about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Request deletion of certain personal data
  • Opt out of certain data-processing activities, where applicable

These rights may not apply to:

  • Publicly available blockchain data
  • Information that Malibu does not control
  • Information required for legal or regulatory compliance
  • Data necessary to maintain the security or functionality of the Services

Requests may be submitted using the contact information provided below.

10Children’s information

The Services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. Malibu does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11Data security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These safeguards are intended to reflect the nature of the Services and the categories of personal data we process.

However, no system, network, or method of electronic transmission is completely secure. While we seek to protect personal data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security, particularly where information is transmitted over the internet or interacts with decentralized networks beyond Malibu’s control.

12Global data processing

The Services rely on globally distributed infrastructure and service providers. As a result, personal data processed in connection with the Services may be transferred to, stored in, or accessed from jurisdictions with data-protection laws that may differ from those of your home jurisdiction. By accessing the Services, you acknowledge that such cross-border processing may occur.

13Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above reflects the effective date of the current version. Continued use of the Services after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.

14Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or requests regarding your personal data, can be sent to team@malibu.tech.
