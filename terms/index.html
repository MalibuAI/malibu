02What Malibu is

Malibu is a network that connects buyers of AI inference with independent providers who host open-source models on their own hardware (primarily Apple Silicon Macs). Buyer usage is currently metered as a daily quota against paid or granted allowance. Providers are settled in USDC when their inference receipts pass verified-model settlement. Prepaid credit balances, USDC top-ups, and on-chain $MALIBU rewards for provider activity are on the roadmap and will become subject to these Terms when they launch. Malibu operates the coordination layer, the console, and the provider desktop app; Malibu does not itself run inference on your prompts — providers do.

The Services are pre-1.0. Features, rate cards, verification rules, token mechanics, and available payment rails are actively evolving. What runs today versus what is planned is tracked on the public status page in the Malibu docs.
