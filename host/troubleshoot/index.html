Troubleshoot Malibu

Most installs are one click. If something stalls, start here — then reach us from the in-app support link or GitHub issues.

Gatekeeper blocked the download

Malibu ships signed and notarized. macOS should open it without extra steps. If you see “Malibu can’t be opened”:

  1. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  2. Scroll to the security section and click Open Anyway for Malibu.
  3. Re-open the .dmg from Downloads and drag Malibu to Applications.

Do not run xattr -cr unless support asks — it bypasses checks we rely on for provider integrity.

Login item / “Open at Login” denied

Malibu registers as a login item so your provider can restart after reboot. If you declined during onboarding:

  1. Open System Settings → General → Login Items.
  2. Enable Malibu under “Open at Login”.
  3. Relaunch Malibu and click Launch Provider again.

Malibu never re-enables this without you clicking — if status shows disabled in the app, use the in-app nudge or Settings above.

First model download stuck

After launch, Malibu downloads the best model for your Mac. Large weights need disk space and a stable connection.

  • Confirm at least 30 GB free on your boot volume (70B-class models need more).
  • Stay on power and avoid sleep until the progress bar completes.
  • Check Malibu’s menu bar status — “Starting…” usually means the bundled provider is still warming up (give it 2–3 minutes).
  • Use Check for Updates in the Malibu menu if the app or provider version looks stale.

If it fails repeatedly, quit Malibu, delete ~/Library/Application Support/Malibu/models, and launch again.

Uninstall

  1. Quit Malibu from the menu bar.
  2. Drag Malibu.app from Applications to Trash.
  3. Remove the login item in System Settings → Login Items if still listed.
  4. Optional — remove local data: ~/Library/Application Support/Malibu and ~/macprovider (CLI track only).
  5. CLI track only: launchctl bootout gui/$(id -u) ~/Library/LaunchAgents/live.streamvc.macprovider.plist

Wallet keys and earnings history live on-chain / coordinator-side — uninstalling the app does not delete your provider identity.
