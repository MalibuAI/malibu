How $MALIBU works
What happens to every $1

Three things happen the moment a prompt clears.

Pay

The Mac gets paid.

70¢ of every dollar goes to the person whose Mac ran the prompt, in USDC, the same day.

70¢ of every $1
Burn

Supply shrinks.

18¢ buys $MALIBU on the open market and burns it. Every prompt leaves a few less tokens in circulation.

18¢ burned
Back

The floor rises.

12¢ goes into a reserve that backs every new $MALIBU. The Macs keeping the network up earn the new tokens.

12¢ to reserve
