For holders

The network is the token.

Every dollar of buyer spend flows through a fixed split: providers get paid, supply burns, reserves grow. Network activity is token demand.

Buyers pay in USDC and never touch $MALIBU. Holders own the network the buyers are using.

What happens to every $1

Three things happen the moment a prompt clears.

Pay

The Mac gets paid.

70¢ of every dollar goes to the person whose Mac ran the prompt, in USDC.

70¢ of every $1
Burn

Supply shrinks.

18¢ buys $MALIBU on the open market and burns it. Every prompt leaves fewer tokens in circulation.

18¢ burned
Back

The floor rises.

12¢ goes into a reserve that backs every new $MALIBU. Macs keeping the network up earn the new tokens.

12¢ to reserve
70¢ + 18¢ + 12¢ = $1 · Burn beats reserve every day the network is used
