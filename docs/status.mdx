## Economics — today vs launch

**Today (marketplace):** providers earn **90%** of gross credits; operator retains 10%. Credits settle weekly in USDC on Base. Details: [Payments & payouts](/guides/payments).

**At token launch (planned):** buyer USDC splits **70%** provider / **12%** reserves / **18%** market-buy-and-burn. Described in the Litepaper flywheel section; not active in production billing yet.
