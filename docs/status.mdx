## Economics — today vs launch

**Today (pre-beta marketplace):** providers earn **90%** of gross credits; operator retains 10%. Credits accrue on the billing ledger. **USDC-on-Base payout ships at public beta** — not while admission is invite-gated pre-beta. Details: [Economics](/guides/economics), [Payments & payouts](/guides/payments).

**At token launch (planned):** buyer USDC splits **70%** provider / **12%** reserves / **18%** market-buy-and-burn. Described in the Litepaper flywheel section; not active in production billing yet.

## Verification primitives

| Primitive | Status | Proves | Does not prove |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Signed receipts | **Live** | Provider key signed `(prompt, output, model_hash, timestamp)` | Forward-pass fidelity, hardware attestation |
| PoMW (memory-hard walk) | **Prototype** | Model residency + memory-substrate rate | Inference equivalence, routing honesty |
| TOPLOC | **Planned v1** | Served completion on attested weights | Pre-buyer readiness |

Full trust boundaries: [Security & trust model](/network/security). Hardware attestation is **not** live (`nodes_hardware_attested` is 0 on the public snapshot).
