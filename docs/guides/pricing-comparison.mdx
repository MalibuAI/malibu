# Pricing comparison

> Evidence for buyer-cost claims in the [Litepaper](/litepaper). **Last reviewed:** 11 November 2026.

Savings depend on the model. Malibu is not uniformly cheaper. This page compares a dated snapshot of the Malibu rate card with public OpenRouter list prices for the same model family.

<Note>
  Buyers have no payment method today. Usage runs against a daily token quota and is metered at these rates. The rates show what Malibu lists, not what you are billed today. See [Get an API key → Quota and billing](/getting-started/api-key#quota-and-billing).
</Note>

## Live Malibu pricing

```
GET https://api.malibu.tech/v1/rate-card
```

Rates are credits per million tokens, and `usd_per_million_credits` converts them to USD (it is `1.0` in this snapshot, so 1,000,000 credits = $1.00). For the full formula, see [Metering & billing](/network/metering-and-billing). For economic regimes, see [Economics](/guides/economics).

<Warning>
  A cheaper rate-card row does not mean the model can take requests today. Check `GET /v1/models` (with a key) or `GET /v1/stats/models` (without one). Quantization, latency, and receipt semantics also differ from OpenRouter, even when the family name matches. OpenRouter's `/models` price is one listed price per model. Individual OpenRouter endpoints, and OpenRouter `:batch` variants, can be cheaper or more expensive.
</Warning>

## How to rerun the comparison

1. Download `https://api.malibu.tech/v1/rate-card` and record `generated_at` and `version`.
2. Download OpenRouter (or another aggregator's) list prices and record the date.
3. Convert Malibu credits: `usd_per_mtok = credits_per_mtok / 1e6 * usd_per_million_credits`.
4. Compare the same token mix. If your agent replays context with a [sticky conversation tag](/guides/sticky-conversations), include `prompt_cache_hit_rate_per_mtok`.
5. Leave out closed flagship models that have no OSS equivalent.

## Default row (no model match)

A request for a model with no matching rate-card row bills at the `default` row: **$0.50 / $1.00** per 1M prompt/completion tokens. That is usually **more expensive** than aggregator OSS list prices. Use a model with its own priced row.

## When Malibu is not cheaper

- **Rows no provider is serving.** A price without supply saves nothing.
- **Two rows.** In this snapshot, Gemma 4 26B A4B and gpt-oss-20b are above OpenRouter list, and Qwen3 32B is even. Gemma's OpenRouter list price comes from one low-priced provider; the median across its 15 OpenRouter providers is $0.081 / $0.30, which Malibu's $0.06 / $0.24 undercuts.
- **Latency and availability.** Requests route to consumer Macs, not dedicated GPU clusters.
- **Confidential compute.** Providers read prompts in plaintext, with no TEE. This page compares price only.
