# Economics

> Canonical split between **what providers earn today** and **what the token launch changes**. **Last reviewed:** 16 August 2026.

Never mix the two regimes in one sentence.

| Regime | Status | Provider share | Buyer pays in | Settlement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Marketplace credits** | **Live** | **90%** of gross credits (10% operator) | Credits → USDC | Weekly on Base, Monday 00:00 UTC |
| **Token flywheel (BME)** | **Planned v0/v1** | **70%** of buyer USDC | $MALIBU + USDC (design) | 12% reserves / 18% buy-and-burn |

Source for live numbers: `GET https://api.malibu.tech/v1/rate-card` (`provider_share_bps: 9000` on every row as of 29 July 2026). Formula: [Metering & billing](/network/metering-and-billing). Payouts: [Payments & payouts](/guides/payments).

## Live marketplace

```
providerCredits = grossCredits × 9000 ÷ 10,000
```

Buyers pay **gross** credits. The 90/10 split is a provider/operator split, not a buyer discount.

Credits convert at `usd_per_million_credits` on the rate card (**1.0** on the 29 July 2026 card). Model-specific prompt/completion rates override the default 500k / 1M credits per million tokens.

Admission is **invite-gated pre-beta**. Earnings are not a public "$X/year per Mac" figure — use `/providers/{id}/earnings` after you serve traffic.

## Planned token launch

When (and only when) token economics activate:

- **70¢** of each buyer dollar to the serving provider
- **12¢** to protocol-owned USDC reserves
- **18¢** market-buy-and-burn of $MALIBU

That split **replaces** 90/10. It is not additive. Effective date: TBD on [Network status](/status).

Illustrative Year-5 earnings tables in the Litepaper assume this regime plus a token price. They are **not** live marketplace payouts. See [Provider economics](/guides/provider-economics).
