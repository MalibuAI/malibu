# Provider economics

> Assumptions behind earnings tables in the [Litepaper](/litepaper). **Last reviewed:** 16 August 2026.

The Litepaper quotes annual earnings ranges (e.g. Mac Studio Ultra **$10,000–$13,000/year** at $MALIBU = $2). Those numbers are **illustrative scenarios** from tokenomics modeling — not live marketplace payouts and not guarantees.

<Warning>
  **Illustrative, not guaranteed.** Actual earnings depend on buyer demand, uptime, model tier, thermal limits, token price, and whether you are on marketplace credits (live) vs token emission (planned v0).
</Warning>

## Two income regimes

| Regime | Status | What providers earn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Marketplace credits** | **Live** | 90% of gross credits on billed inference; weekly USDC on Base |
| **Token emission + flywheel** | **Planned v0/v1** | PoMW mining rewards + 70% of buyer USDC at token launch |

Never mix the two in one planning spreadsheet without labeling the regime.

## Live marketplace economics (today)

Source: [Payments & payouts](/guides/payments), [Metering & billing](/network/metering-and-billing).

```
providerCredits = grossCredits × 90%
```

Earnings scale with:

- Requests routed to your Mac (pool size, tier, uptime)
- Model served (rate card per model)
- Fault rate (invalid/missing receipts → zero credits)

There is **no** public fixed "$X/year per Mac tier" on the live rail — compute from your served volume using `/providers/{id}/earnings`.

## Hardware reality checks

From Phase 1 mining research (`mining-prototype-phase1.md`):

- **Fanless Air** may sustain ~**76%** of peak hashrate over 30 minutes — apply a duty-cycle discount for 24/7 planning.
- **Mining at full throttle** can be hostile to daily interactive use; cooperative scheduling yields to buyer requests (~50 ms at v1 design target).
- **Unified memory** caps which catalog models a tier can warm. Llama 3.3 70B and gpt-oss-120B need Studio Ultra-class RAM; they route when those Macs join.

## Sensitivity checklist

Before treating any annual number as a forecast, stress-test:

1. **Uptime** — lid-close sleep drops WebSocket; see [Payments → provisional vs pinned](/guides/payments#provisional-vs-pinned).
2. **Pool competition** — more providers → shared demand unless buyer volume grows proportionally.
3. **Token price** — emission income is highly sensitive; marketplace USDC income is not (today).
4. **Thermal / duty cycle** — sustained load ≠ burst benchmark peak.
5. **Receipt quality** — faults zero credits; see [Verified model settlement](/operations/verified-model-settlement).
