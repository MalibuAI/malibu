# Provider economics

> What drives provider credits today, and the assumptions behind the illustrative token-launch scenarios referenced by the [Litepaper](/litepaper). **Last reviewed:** 23 September 2026.

Earlier versions of the Litepaper quoted per-Mac annual figures and hardware-payback lines from tokenomics scenario modeling. Those figures were removed on 23 September 2026. The scenario inputs below are kept for context. They are **illustrative** and depend on a planned token launch and an assumed token price. They are not live marketplace payouts, not projected earnings, not investment returns, and not guarantees.

<Warning>
  **Illustrative, not guaranteed.** What a Mac actually earns depends on buyer demand, uptime, routing tier, which models it can fit, thermal limits, receipt quality, and which regime applies: marketplace credits (live) or token emission (planned). Nothing on this page is a forecast or a recommendation to buy hardware or tokens.
</Warning>

## Two income regimes

| Regime | Status | What providers earn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Marketplace credits** | **Live (pre-beta)** | 90% of gross credits on verified, billed inference. Credits accrue on the ledger; USDC-on-Base payout is planned for public beta |
| **Token emission + flywheel** | **Planned** | Emission rewards plus 70% of buyer USDC at token launch |

An off-chain reward ledger for verified work is kept in pre-beta as a preview. It cannot be withdrawn and is not marketplace income. See [Economics → reward ledger](/guides/economics#reward-ledger-preview).

If you put both regimes in one planning spreadsheet, label which regime each row belongs to.

## Live marketplace economics (today)

Sources: [Payments & payouts](/guides/payments) and [Metering & billing](/network/metering-and-billing).

```
providerCredits = grossCredits × 9000 ÷ 10,000
```

Rate arithmetic, not a forecast: from the [live rate card](/guides/economics#live-rate-card), 1 million completion tokens on `qwen3-32b` is $0.22 gross, and $0.198 of that is provider credit. The same volume on `qwen3-8b` is $0.027 gross and $0.0243 provider credit. How many tokens your Mac actually serves is the unknown, and it depends on buyer demand.

What moves your credits:

- **Traffic routed to your Mac.** This depends on buyer demand, pool competition, uptime, and routing tier. Provisional providers route at reduced weight; trusted and pinned route at full weight.
- **The model you serve.** Rates differ by model row on the rate card, by up to about 80× on completion tokens.
- **Settlement.** Only rows with a valid, on-time receipt are payable; see [Verified model settlement](/operations/verified-model-settlement).
- **Buyer pricing in pre-beta.** Public buyers use a free daily quota, so today's demand is not paid demand.

The live rail has **no** public fixed "$X/year per Mac" figure. Read your own served volume and earnings in the Malibu app.

## Hardware reality checks

- **Unified memory limits which rows a Mac can serve.** The signed autotune feed lists a minimum RAM for each row. Examples: `meta-llama/llama-3.2-3b-instruct` needs 4 GB, `qwen3-8b` 12 GB, `openai/gpt-oss-20b` 24 GB, `qwen2.5-coder-32b-instruct` and `qwen3-32b` 48 GB, `z-ai/glm-4.5-air` 80 GB, and `openai/gpt-oss-120b` 90 GB. Higher-rate rows route only when a Mac that fits them is online.
- **Sustained load is not burst peak.** Sustained throughput is lower than a short benchmark peak. Apply a duty-cycle discount for 24/7 planning. See [Benchmarks & methodology](/network/benchmarks-and-methodology).
- **Interactive use.** The mining design targets yielding to buyer requests within about 50 ms. That is a v1 design target, not a shipped measurement.

## Sensitivity checklist

Before you treat any annual number as a forecast, stress-test it against:

1. **Uptime.** Lid-close sleep drops the WebSocket; see [Payments → why your Mac might drop](/guides/payments#why-your-mac-might-drop-mid-window).
2. **Pool competition.** More providers share the same demand unless buyer volume grows with them.
3. **Paid demand.** Public buyer usage is unpaid in pre-beta.
4. **Token price.** Emission income is highly sensitive to it. Marketplace credits are priced in USD on the rate card.
5. **Thermal limits and duty cycle.** Sustained load is lower than a burst benchmark peak.
6. **Receipt quality.** Missing or invalid receipts pay zero; see [Verified model settlement](/operations/verified-model-settlement).
