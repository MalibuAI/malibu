Signed & verifiable

Every response is signed on-node. You can check it yourself.

You're only billed when the receipt verifies — settlement refunds anything it can't.

A receipt proves who served the response and that it wasn't altered — not that the answer is correct. Forward-pass attestation (TOPLOC) is planned for v1.

How receipts work →
Ed25519 signature check — 

          
Live demo: we generate a keypair, sign a sample receipt tuple, and verify the signature in your browser — the Ed25519 primitive at the core of every Malibu receipt. Production receipts add RFC 8785 canonicalization; verify a real one with malibu-verify.
